Built around songs, not files

Backing tracks often end up in Downloads, tabs in another folder, and the right version somewhere in a browser bookmark. JamCrate turns that collection into songs that are ready to play.

One song, one folder

JamCrate copies the backing track, tab and song data into one managed folder. Your library no longer depends on files scattered across the disk.

Track and tab open together

Select a song and its audio, tab and notes are right there with it. Sheets open inside the app — PDF, plain text, chord charts — and the player keeps time-stretched audio on screen while you play.

Guitar Pro files open as tab

Guitar Pro 3–5 files render right in the sheet pane — one track at a time, fitted to the window, no Guitar Pro installation needed. Want the original arrangement? One click opens the file in Guitar Pro.

Mark and repeat any section

Add In and Out markers around a solo, verse or difficult passage. Play the marked sections in sequence or loop one until it works.

Links and notes ride with the song

The YouTube original you learned from, a lyrics page, a reminder about the capo — each song keeps its web links and one note, visible right in the player.

Setlists and favorites

Keep rehearsal material and performance setlists in playing order. Mark favorites and move through a set without searching for the next file.

Open the set on your phone

Phone menu — QR code. Scan it on a phone in the same Wi-Fi: the set opens there live, the sound streams from the Mac and nothing gets stored on the phone. Solo loops run right on the phone screen, and the phone works as a remote for the Mac. Your music never leaves your network.

Import without the filing work

Drop in individual files or a whole folder. JamCrate pairs tracks with matching tabs and asks only when something is unclear. Audio can also be imported from a supported video link.

Your library stays yours

No account and no cloud storage required. Your music remains on your Mac and can be moved by copying the JamCrate library folder.

Back up the complete library

Create a single backup containing your tracks, tabs, markers, setlists and rig files. JamCrate verifies the archive before reporting success — and restores every song complete or not at all.

MIDI preset automationexperimental

What I am building: JamCrate switches presets on your MIDI processor when a song starts and when you cross a marked section — you keep playing, the tone changes itself. It does not work yet, and I am not naming devices.

Before I commit time to it, I want to hear from players: what pedal do you have, and would you use this at all? The shape of the feature is decided by that feedback — email at the bottom of the page.

Roadmap

Two things are on the road right now — and the first one only gets built if players say it is needed.

MIDI presets on your pedal — do you even want this?

What I am working on is reliable preset switching on real gear. Today it does not work and no device is promised. First I want to know whether anyone actually wants it: what you play through, and what would have to work to be worth doing. Tell me at beta@jamcrate.app.

Export to a plain player

Your set as a folder any player understands: full tracks numbered in set order, marked solos cut into separate audio files, ready M3U playlists. AirDrop it to your phone — and play in whatever you already like.

Installation

  1. Open the DMG and drag JamCrate to Applications.
  2. The beta is not notarized yet. On the first launch, Control-click JamCrate and choose Open.
  3. After the first launch, it opens normally.

If macOS still blocks the application, run this command once:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/JamCrate.app
