Drop a backing track. Hit Enter. Play.

JamCrate keeps your backing tracks, tabs and song settings together — ready when you want to play.

macOS 14+ · Apple Silicon & Intel · free beta

What it does

Built around the way hobbyists actually practice.

One-Enter import

Drag a pile of mp3s, tabs and PDFs onto the window. Anything unambiguous is added and ready to play; anything doubtful asks exactly one short question.

Tracks + tabs together

Audio and its tab live in one folder and open together. Text, chord sheets and PDFs get fragment-aware autoscroll while you play.

Solo markers & loops

Mark a solo, loop it, drill it. Drag markers to move them, double-click to delete. Practice the hard eight bars forty times without touching the pedal.

Your pedal follows the song

MIDI preset switching for the M-VAVE Tank and Line 6 Helix: enter a marked fragment, the tone changes. Beta testers get first dibs on new devices.

Backups that double-click back

One .zip carries your whole crate — audio, tabs, markers — verified before it claims success. Move to a new Mac by copying the folder.

Everything stays local

No account, no analytics, no network calls from your library. If the website disappears tomorrow, your crate keeps working.

Get the beta

The beta is the full app, free, and your practice habits shape what ships. During beta the MIDI-pedal layer is the planned paid feature (one-time, around €29); everything else stays free.

Install (20 seconds)

  1. Open the .dmg and drag JamCrate onto the Applications shortcut.
  2. Launch it the first time with a right-click → Open (Gatekeeper likes verified developers; this beta is signed ad-hoc). Then it opens normally.
  3. If macOS still refuses: run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/JamCrate.app once.
